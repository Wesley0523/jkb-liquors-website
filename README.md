# JKB Liquors Website MVP

## Project Overview

The JKB Liquors Website MVP is a modern e-commerce platform designed to help JKB Liquors move from traditional in-store and card payments into an online ordering system.

The goal of this project is to create a simple, user-friendly online shopping experience where customers can browse products, place orders, make payments, and collect their purchases from the store.

This MVP focuses on building a strong foundation that can later be expanded with delivery services, customer accounts, advanced inventory management, and AI-powered business tools.

---

# Business Objective

JKB Liquors currently operates successfully through physical sales and point-of-sale payments. The purpose of this website is to create an additional sales channel that allows customers to:

- Browse available liquor products online
- View product information and pricing
- Place orders before arriving at the store
- Complete secure online payments
- Collect orders conveniently from the store

The system is designed to improve customer convenience while helping the business manage orders more efficiently.

# Development Questions We Asked

Before building the system, we considered important business and technical questions.

## Business Questions

### How can we increase sales without changing the current business model?

The initial solution was to create an online ordering platform while keeping collection as the first delivery method.

---

### What problem does the website solve?

The website reduces customer waiting time, improves convenience, and creates a digital sales channel for customers who prefer ordering online.

---

### Should delivery be included immediately?

We decided not to include delivery in the first version.

Reasons:

- Additional logistics complexity
- Additional operational costs
- Need for delivery management
- Easier MVP testing with store collection

Delivery can be added after the ordering system is proven.

---

### How do we make ordering alcohol responsible and compliant?

The system needs an age verification process before customers can purchase alcohol.

The platform must support responsible alcohol sales practices and follow South African regulations.

---
